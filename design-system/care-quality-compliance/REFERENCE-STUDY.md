# Reference study, 3 October 2026

## Reviewed references

- Awwwards Health & Fitness gallery: https://www.awwwards.com/websites/health-fitness/
- Allia Health Group entry: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/allia-health-group and live website https://alliahealth.co/ . Reviewed live desktop rendering: oversized editorial serif, full-frame human photography and restrained navigation. The useful principle is pairing authority in typography with humanity in imagery.
- Impilo entry: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/impilo-health-care and https://impilo.health/ . Inspected headings and DOM; the captured visual remained on its loading screen, so this was not a complete visual review.
- Godly: https://godly.website/ (redirects to Recent). Inspected its design feed and portfolio preview images. The Sam Cheng portfolio preview pairs unusually large serif type with a single physical focal object. The useful principle is a memorable central composition and a deliberate type hierarchy.

These references informed composition and interaction principles. No reference site’s code, branding, copy or imagery is copied.

## Applied direction

1. The hero uses a quiet, full-frame film of hands held in care, with a licensed local asset and a legible flat overlay.
2. Libre Caslon Display and Source Sans 3 replace the previous geometric sans pairing across all routes.
3. One sustained scroll sequence explains the consultancy process through a drawn route and four stages. Native scroll is preserved. Mobile and reduced-motion versions reveal every stage without pinning.
4. The consultancy finder and training planner provide useful decisions, then carry those choices to the enquiry form. No fictional data, scores or outcomes are presented.
