# Typography refinement: 4 October 2026

The user asked for a subtle change toward IBM.com and Apple.com typography, removal of the decorative bullet treatment and retention of the green/lavender/sage palette.

The live homepages were inspected in Chromium. IBM's visible headings used IBM Plex Sans at weight 300, with weight 400 body text. Apple's site used SF Pro Text and SF Pro Display, with restrained tracking and a clear hierarchy. These observations informed the refinement; this is not a reproduction of either site's brand or layout.

The site now uses a single locally hosted IBM Plex Sans variable font for display, body, navigation and actions. The hero uses weight 300; section headings are regular and controls use medium weights. Unused Bricolage files were removed, including the old social-image font. The sharing image now uses a static IBM Plex Sans instance. Its OFL licence and attribution are included. No Apple font files were copied or redistributed.

Decorative dots in the hero kicker, homepage principles and practice notes were removed. Selection remains visibly expressed through line/border treatments and accessible state attributes. Meaningful step numbers, checks, disclosure controls and form labels remain.

Sources: [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/), [Apple](https://www.apple.com/), [IBM Plex source and licence](https://github.com/IBM/plex).
