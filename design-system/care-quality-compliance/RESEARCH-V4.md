## Design decisions implemented

1. **Retain the accepted video and composition.** The hero now uses a neutral scrim, preserving the natural skin tones instead of applying a green cast.
2. **Replace arrow capsules with text-led actions.** One contiguous rectangular target, readable IBM Plex Sans, a short colour-panel movement on hover and an independent focus outline. No opacity fades. Disclosure, selection, back and film controls keep their meaningful symbols.
3. **Separate the type roles.** Bricolage Grotesque retains the accepted expressive display character. Locally hosted IBM Plex Sans replaces Hanken for navigation, controls and longer reading. Both families use OFL licences supplied with the project.
4. **Create a practice index.** Six direct, server-rendered links sit alongside original line studies. Pointer or keyboard focus selects the related illustration. The folio follows native scrolling on large screens. Focus never moves automatically. Mobile and no-JavaScript visitors can reach every service without using the preview.
5. **Use a coherent colour vocabulary.** Ink and chalk form the base; vermilion marks emphasis and actions; pale blue and apricot distinguish working spaces. The scattered green hex values are replaced by shared CSS tokens. Functional error colours remain meaningful.
6. **Make motion part of the explanation.** The drawn care journey remains. Illustrated accent lines draw as the selected practice changes; training sheets settle into place; review diagrams move subtly with scrolling; the procurement line progresses along its route. Reduced motion renders static, readable compositions and mobile does not pin these scenes.
7. **Retain the service tools.** Review-area tabs, tender stages, the full training catalogue and planner, independent-assessment outline, family questions and allowlisted enquiry handoffs all remain available.

The reference sites' code, imagery, copy, marks and proprietary typefaces are not used. No speculative client results, awards, accreditations or testimonials are added. A distinctive website alone does not guarantee an award, search position or enquiry rate.
